### Palagoma — Odd Okoddo & Ogoya Nengo
**风格**: experimental / world fusion / folktronica / electronic / African traditional
**评分**: 8/10  ⭐⭐⭐⭐
**来源**: [RootsWorld](https://rootsworld.com/reviews/palagoma-26.shtml)
